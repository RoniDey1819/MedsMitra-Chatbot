Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your first order.

How do I order medicines online from MedsMitra?
Search for your medicine or upload a prescription, add items to your cart, choose a delivery slot and pay online or on delivery. You'll get order tracking from checkout to your doorstep.
Can I buy all kinds of medicines and health products here?
Yes — over-the-counter medicines, prescription drugs (with a valid prescription), vitamins, personal care, baby care, ayurveda and medical devices are all available.
Is online medicine delivery safe?
All medicines are sourced from licensed distributors and shipped in tamper-evident, temperature-appropriate packaging, with batch and expiry details visible before you buy.
How can I buy prescribed medicines?
Upload a photo or PDF of a valid doctor's prescription at checkout. Our registered pharmacists verify it before your order is dispatched.
Can I track my delivery?
Yes — every order includes real-time tracking from our order confirmation email or your Profile > Orders page.
Does MedsMitra offer express delivery?
Express delivery within 2-4 hours is available in select cities for in-stock medicines, shown at checkout when eligible.
